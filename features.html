App Features

MoRoute is a mobile application designed to enhance road safety and security for travellers in Nigeria. The app integrates incident reporting, proactive ahead-of-route alerts, emergency SOS features, and resilience in low connectivity or low battery conditions. It ensures that travellers can receive and send alerts even in challenging environments.
